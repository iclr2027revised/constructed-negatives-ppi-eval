The distributed statistics are author computations, not redistributions of source interaction databases. No evidence was found that the cited terms prohibit their publication. Missing exact source versions limit reconstruction; they do not automatically block release of original aggregate research results. The private decision ledger is deliberately outside this repository.

Modifications to Gold/UniProt: length eligibility filtering, synthetic negative generation, frozen endpoint-shift evaluation, sequence normalization/rekeying, metadata stripping and compression. Row multiplicity and original evaluation labels are preserved. All source attribution is retained; anonymity is not a reason to remove third-party credit.
